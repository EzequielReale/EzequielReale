### 🧑‍💻 About Me

- 🎓 **B.S. in Computer Systems** from Universidad Nacional de La Plata (UNLP) *(...survived the thesis defense)*.
- 💼 **Full Stack Developer & IT Consultant**.
- 📝 **Teaching Assistant** at UNLP for *"Fundamentals of Data Organization"* (B/B+ trees, extensible hashing, and disk I/O performance) and *"Database Design"* (relational algebra, schema normalization, and SQL).
- 📚 Former **Head of Practical Assignments** in *"Databases I & II"* at UCALP.
- ⚔️ On a daily crusade to convince the world that **Excel is not a database**.
- 💬 Ask me about: data modeling, decoupled architectures, or why proper database indexing will save your day.
- ⚡ A grandma navigates Instagram better than I do.

### 💼 What I Do

- 🏗️ **Full Stack Dev at Ingeniería y Construcciones Alsina**: Building management system modules (Spring Boot, Angular, Django, MySQL), KPI dashboards, and internal business process engines.
- 🚛 **IT Consultant at Logística Diesel (Grupo Guazzaroni Greco)**: Leading TMS (Transport Management System) implementation and operational migration from spreadsheets to unified platforms.
- 👨‍🏫 **University Teaching at UNLP**: Trying to ensure students don't rate us a 0 on course surveys.

---

### 🚀 Featured Projects

- 🧠 **CasER 4.0**: A web-based CASE tool that automatically generates conceptual, logical, and relational database designs from natural language specifications while ensuring end-to-end traceability. Adopted as educational software for undergraduate and postgraduate courses at UNLP, developed as my degree thesis, and published in scientific proceedings. *(React, React Flow, Node.js, Express, MUI, Google Drive API)*.
- 🤖 **ALSINITO**: A real-time road mechanical assistance WhatsApp bot. Features tailored workflows for drivers and mechanics, automatic retries, delivery tracking, and a decoupled REST API architecture. Easily extendable to other organizational areas *(Python, Django, MySQL, WhatsApp API)*.
- 🐴 **[CEDICA](https://github.com/EzequielReale/Proyecto-De-Software-2024-CEDICA)**: Full management system and public portal developed for a therapeutic equestrian non-profit organization. *(UNLP Software Capstone Project)*.
